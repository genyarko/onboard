The "First Day" at a new software job is usually a mix of excitement and "Where do I even start?" 😵‍💫

Most of us have faced the "Onboarding Tax":
❌ Outdated documentation.
❌ 10,000+ files with zero architectural map.
❌ The "Why" behind critical code buried in years of PR history.
❌ Constant interruptions to senior devs for basic context.

That’s why I built **Onboard** — a VS Code extension designed to act as your personal "Repo Oracle" from day one. 🛠️✨

Powered by **IBM Bob**, Onboard doesn't just look at the file you have open. It synthesizes the *entire* repository’s history, structure, and intent to give you:

🔍 **Repo X-Ray:** Instant architecture diagrams and "weird part" detection.
❓ **Why Is This Here?:** Right-click any line to see the historical business reason behind it (cross-referencing Git & PR history).
📅 **Day-N Plan:** A personalized 5-day roadmap tailored to your specific role and experience level.
✅ **Starter Tasks:** Beginner-friendly contributions identified by AI to get you shipping on day one.

We leveraged IBM Bob’s massive context window to bridge the gap between "code on the screen" and "the soul of the repository."
